Canada's labour market continues to face shortages in several key sectors. Here's where the opportunity is strongest for international candidates right now.

## Healthcare

Personal support workers, nursing assistants, and allied health professionals remain in consistent demand across every province, driven by an aging population and ongoing capacity pressure in the healthcare system.

## Hospitality

Restaurants and hotels across Canada continue to rebuild staffing levels, creating steady demand for chefs, servers, and food service managers — particularly in urban centres and tourist regions.

## Retail

Retailers are looking for reliable store-level talent, from sales associates to store managers, especially ahead of peak seasonal periods.
