Finding a job in Canada as an international candidate involves more moving parts than a typical domestic job search — but the path is well-defined once you understand each stage.

## 1. Confirm Your Eligibility

Before applying anywhere, understand which immigration and work permit pathways apply to your situation. Requirements vary depending on your country of origin, qualifications, and the type of role you're targeting.

## 2. Build a Canadian-Style Resume

Canadian employers expect a specific resume format — concise, achievement-focused, and free of personal details like photos or age. Tailor your resume to each role rather than sending a generic version.

## 3. Work With a Licensed Recruiter

A recruitment partner who understands both the Canadian labour market and immigration process can significantly shorten your search — connecting you directly with employers who are open to hiring internationally.

## 4. Secure a Job Offer

Most employer-specific work permits require a formal job offer, and in many cases, a Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA) from the employer.

## 5. Apply for Your Work Permit

Once your job offer is confirmed, you'll apply for the appropriate work permit through Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC).
